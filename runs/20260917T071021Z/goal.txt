seed: spread-column
`evals.py` prints beside the median of each measure that decides a comparison between two runs of the set, `requests`, `cost_usd` and `seconds`, its spread over the case's runs: a column named after the median with `_spread`, right after it in `COLUMNS`, holding the lowest and the highest value of the runs as `min-max`, each written as the median is, a whole number without a decimal point and any other rounded to six decimals, the two ends the same when the runs agree or there is one run; a run that lacks the measure is left out as it is from the median, and the cell is empty when no run has it. Nothing else in the table changes, and `runs.py` needs nothing. From the review: a value in a record's `numbers.json` that is not a finite number, `NaN` or an infinity, which JSON accepts, is a measure the run lacks, left out of the median, the spread and the summed cost alike, and the table still prints; and the module docstring of `evals.py` says the spread is printed beside those three medians as `min-max`. The tests in `test_evals.py` whose docstring names this seed define the behaviour.
