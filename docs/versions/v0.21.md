# v0.21: the loop, derived

The morning after v0.19 shipped, everything the gate did not hold had drifted and nothing it held had: README five versions stale, a delivered seed still open, a done seed at spec for a day. The owner named the causes beyond the prose itself -- a session compressed round after round, and a workflow that exists only as text, so a step is taken only if the agent notices it. The answer is the factory's own: the next step is derived and printed, never remembered, and a session is one step long. This version builds that in two moves, in order: `gate.py`, which holds every reader the derivation needs, is split into three modules with one responsibility each and failures that say what failed, because a quality review found it braided and silent; then the loop becomes a table of rules over one snapshot of facts, and `gate.py next` prints the one step that comes next.

## Changelog

- The gate is three modules with one responsibility each. `vault.py` reads the vault as git holds it: every file's text and every note as a `Note` with its fields, its body and the error when it could not be read, each read once; `repo.py` reads and writes the repository behind one `Result(code, out, err)` and readers that raise `RepoError` naming the command rather than answering with nothing; `gate.py` checks and commands over both, its problems typed, its `render` returning text. A failure is one problem naming its cause -- a tree that is no checkout gets two lines where it got twenty false ones, an unreadable note or template one line and the checks that do not derive from it still run, a test file that does not parse one line and the other files still count, a git that fails during a release refuses it naming the command where `git status` failing once cut a tag on a dirty tree. `gate.py` is 782 lines from 1,105, beside 405 of `vault.py` and 334 of `repo.py`; the fixture's `check` is line-identical and its `render` byte-identical to the gate before. Three builds, $0.29 at the flat rate, two of them landed at the spend cap and taken as the next build's base; two reviews of seventeen findings, every one reproduced and judged, eight of them failures the seed's own Evidence had named and its Goal's list had forgotten, and one the attended agent's own wording had licensed. `ruff` formats and checks all three at a hundred columns. The wikilink out of the vault that passed since v0.14 resolves to nothing now, which closed a seed of its own.
- The loop is a command. `uv run gate.py next` prints the one step of the development loop that comes next, derived from git and the vault and never remembered: `loop.py` gathers one frozen snapshot of facts through `vault`, `repo` and `host` -- the gate's own problems, the tags, the head and its branch, the version in flight and each of its seeds with the tests naming it, their colour from running exactly those tests, its builds by `Built-By` and whether the latest is reviewed, the release's preconditions, and after a tag the changelog, `main`, the mirror and the installed product -- and answers with the first of thirteen rules that applies, in the loop's order, a fact that could not be read being `None` with a line and never a value that means something else. `repo.py` gains the readers it needs and refuses a root that is not its own top level; `host.py` reads the installed product's version and nothing else. AGENTS.md's loop is that command, and the six prose steps became the four things it cannot derive. Eleven builds, $1.23 at the flat rate; three reviews, twenty-eight findings, every one reproduced and judged, thirteen of them the loop guessing where the Goal said `None` and several taught by the attended agent's own fixtures -- a shim that answered `uv "tool list"`, a repository whose head sat at its own tag, a test that committed a clean tree. Two cold builds read everything and wrote nothing before the cap, which is now [[the-cap-a-cold-read-eats]]; the Goal was rewritten whole and given a build order, and each red run's tree was taken as the next run's base. On this repository `next` answers in sixteen seconds, and its first real answer was the build it was itself waiting for.
