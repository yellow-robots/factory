# NNNN. Title in the imperative

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What forces are in play. Facts and constraints, not opinions. Cite the record (an issue, a
run, a finding) when there is one.

## Decision

What we do. One decision per record. Written in full sentences, in the present tense.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to watch. A record is never edited
after acceptance; a change of mind is a new record that supersedes this one.
