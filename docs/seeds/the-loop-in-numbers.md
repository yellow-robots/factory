## Evidence

2026-09-17: v0.13's note says five builds, about 25 cents and sixteen minutes, four reviews, eight defects and nineteen smells; the attended agent counted each from the records, the trailers and the notes with scripts of its scratch, `builds7.py`, `set7_summary.py`, `analyst_builds.py` and `trailers.py` among them, as for the notes before it. Counted the same way on the day: the eight notes of `docs/reviews/` hold 60 findings, 15 defects and 45 smells, every one reproduced by the attended agent, all in builds whose check was green, the defects all turned into tests, the smells into 23 tests, 2 seeds and 20 judged `none`; of the seven seeds the factory built from a seed's path, six had a green first build and five took a second red commit after a review, four of them after a green first build (analyst-on-api-models). The builder builds what the tests ask, and what the tests do not ask is found by review, so the quality of what the factory makes is set today by who writes the spec and who reviews, the attended agent and its Opus subagents, neither measured. The owner, on the day: what is evaluated is that the factory is a good factory, capabilities are grown and responsibilities transferred as they are measured, and the two alternate without end. The set measures one step, the build, on made cases; what the loop took on the factory's own work, and how much of it the factory did, no program reads.

## Idea

A reader beside `runs.py`, per version and per seed of it: the builds git lists by their trailer, with their records' cost and seconds; the red commits before and after the first build; the reviews, their findings by severity and by what they became; and the commits by who made them, the factory's by their trailer and the attended agent's the rest, so the share of the loop's commits the factory made is a number that moves as responsibilities do. The version note's counts are read from it and no longer counted by hand. It reads the records where records-outside-the-project keeps them and git for the rest, and a step a role takes over later, the analyst's spec or the reviewer's note, shows in it by the trailer its commits carry.
