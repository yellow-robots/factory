## Evidence

2026-09-21, the morning after v0.19 shipped, read by the attended agent cold from `AGENTS.md`. `README.md` opened with `v0.14`, counted 212 tests of 321, named neither `reviewer.py` nor `catch.py`, and described caps two versions gone; nothing reads it, and the loop's step 6 does not name it. `installation-and-surfaces`, value 5, sat `open` and unassigned across five versions with its Idea delivered by v0.15. `the-catch-rate-that-decides-the-role` sat at `spec` for a day with 23 green tests naming it, because `gate.py:624` holds status in one direction. On release day `git merge --ff-only v0.19` took the tag, one commit short of the branch, past a one-line warning. Everything the gate holds -- the vault's shape, a seed's fields, `Built-By`, review judgements, changelog bullets, `AGENTS.md` changed since the tag -- had not drifted. Everything that drifted was held by prose.

The owner, the same day, named two causes beyond the prose itself. A long session is compressed round after round, and an agent's performance deteriorates with each; and the workflow exists only as text, with nothing guiding the agent to the next step, so whether a step is taken is fully dependent on whether the instruction is noticed in context. The factory already treats its own roles this way: a builder and a reviewer are cold sessions, given what they need and nothing to remember.

## Idea

The next step of the loop is derived and printed, never remembered. `gate.py next` reads git and the vault and prints the one step that comes next with the command that takes it, and nothing else: the version in flight; each of its seeds against its Goal, the tests naming it and their colour, the builds since the tag by trailer, its review note and the judgements in it; the changelog bullets and `AGENTS.md` against the last tag; and after the tag, the changelog commit, `main` against the tag, the mirror, the instance's version. `AGENTS.md`'s loop becomes that command, and a session becomes one step long, cold, as the factory's own roles are.

Before a Goal is written this needs an architectural round -- the states, what derives each, and the patterns that keep the code maintainable -- which is the owner's condition of 2026-09-21. [[the-readme-the-gate-never-reads]] and [[the-status-the-tests-already-know]] are two of its lines and go with it.
