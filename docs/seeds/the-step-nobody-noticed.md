## Evidence

2026-09-21, the morning after v0.19 shipped, read by the attended agent cold from `AGENTS.md`. `README.md` opened with `v0.14`, counted 212 tests of 321, named neither `reviewer.py` nor `catch.py`, and described caps two versions gone; nothing reads it, and the loop's step 6 does not name it. `installation-and-surfaces`, value 5, sat `open` and unassigned across five versions with its Idea delivered by v0.15. `the-catch-rate-that-decides-the-role` sat at `spec` for a day with 23 green tests naming it, because `gate.py:624` holds status in one direction. On release day `git merge --ff-only v0.19` took the tag, one commit short of the branch, past a one-line warning. Everything the gate holds -- the vault's shape, a seed's fields, `Built-By`, review judgements, changelog bullets, `AGENTS.md` changed since the tag -- had not drifted. Everything that drifted was held by prose.

The owner, the same day, named two causes beyond the prose itself. A long session is compressed round after round, and an agent's performance deteriorates with each; and the workflow exists only as text, with nothing guiding the agent to the next step, so whether a step is taken is fully dependent on whether the instruction is noticed in context. The factory already treats its own roles this way: a builder and a reviewer are cold sessions, given what they need and nothing to remember.

The architectural round, 2026-09-21. Three shapes were put to the owner: rules as data over one snapshot of facts in a module of its own, with the gate's readers made public; the same after splitting `gate.py` first; or more imperative checks inside `gate.py`. The owner chose the split first, as [[the-gate-in-three]], and the rules-as-data shape after it. The split is built: `vault.py` reads the vault into `Note`s, `repo.py` reads git into `Result`s and raises `RepoError` where it used to answer with nothing, and `gate.py` checks over both. This seed is the second move. Two smaller questions were put and not answered, and are decided here as assumptions the owner may overturn at review: the colour of a seed's tests is learned by running those tests alone, seconds rather than the suite's minute; and the acts after a tag -- the changelog's commit, `main`, the mirror, the instance -- are rows of the loop, each read fail-soft.

## Goal

The next step of the loop is derived and printed, never remembered.

**`loop.py` holds one snapshot and one table.** `Facts` is a frozen dataclass, gathered once by `gather(root)` through `vault.Vault.read`, `repo.tags`, `repo.tests`, `repo.builds`, `repo.trailers` and `repo.git`, and never mutated; `RULES` is an ordered tuple of `Rule(name, applies, step)`, each `applies` a predicate over `Facts` and each `step` a function of `Facts` returning the text to print; `next_step(facts) -> Step(name, text)` is the first rule that applies, read top to bottom, and the order of the tuple is the order of the loop. A rule is one row; adding a step is adding a row; nothing in a rule reads a file or runs a command. Rules as data over a functional core, with the reading at the edge.

**What `Facts` holds**, by field, every one read once by `gather` and `None` where it could not be read, with one line in `unknown` saying why -- never a guess, never a value that means something else. `root`: the root. `problems`: the gate's own, `gate.problems_check(root, vault)` over the one Vault `gather` read, as the lines the gate prints. `tags` and `highest`: the tags and the highest of them. `head`: the head's hash. `branch` and `attached`: the branch the head is on and `True`; or, detached, the one branch whose tip is the head and `False`; or `None` and `False` when there is none. `in_flight`: the one version note whose name is no tag, or `None` -- and `None` when the tags are unknown, not every note. `seeds`: one `SeedFacts` per seed whose `version` is the version in flight, in name order -- `name`; `status`; `tests`, the ids of the tests naming it, `seed: <name>` in a docstring, as `module.Class.method` or `module.Class`, read from `repo.tests` and from nothing else, `loop.py` walking no source of its own; `colour`, `green` or `red` from `repo.run_tests` over exactly those ids, `none` when no test names it, and `None` with a line when the run could not happen; `builds`, one `Build(commit, stamp)` per commit since the highest tag whose subject is the seed's name **and** whose `Built-By` trailer names a run -- a commit without the trailer is no build -- oldest first; `reviewed`, whether the Vault holds `docs/reviews/<stamp>.md` for the latest build, `None` when there is none. `agents_changed`, `bullets`, `dirty`: whether `AGENTS.md` changed since the highest tag, whether the version note has a changelog bullet, and the uncommitted paths. After a tag: `after_tag`, whether the highest tag is at the head or exactly one commit behind it; `changelog_current`, whether `CHANGELOG.md`'s first `## ` heading names the highest tag whole, `## <tag>:` or `## <tag>` alone; `main_at_head`, whether `refs/heads/main` is at the head; `mirror_at_head`, whether `origin`'s `main` is at the head, asked only when `after_tag`, through `repo.ls_remote` with a timeout, and `None` before a tag with no line, since no row reads it then; `instance_version`, the product's version from `uv tool list`, its colour escapes stripped. `unknown`: one line per fact that is `None`, `<what>: <why>`; `next` prints `unknown: <line>` after the step for each. A directory that is no git checkout, or one git answers about another top level for, has every git fact `None` with one line, and the gate's own problems as they are.

**The rows, in this order**; a row whose facts are unknown is skipped, and the unknown is printed after the step. (1) The gate has problems: `fix: <the first problem, as the gate prints it>` -- whatever else is true, after a tag as before one. (2) After a tag: the first of `commit CHANGELOG.md`, `fast-forward main: git merge --ff-only heads/<tag>`, `push: git push origin main --tags`, `install: uv tool install --reinstall dist/factory-<number>-py3-none-any.whl` where `<number>` is the tag without its `v`, else `<tag> is out: open the next version` -- the `else` holding when an act's fact is unknown, so the instance unknown still ends in `is out`. (3) No version in flight: `open a version: write docs/versions/vX.md and set version: vX on a seed`. (4) The version in flight names no seed: `promote a seed to <version>`. Rows 5 to 11 are a seed's, and the seed stepped is the first in name order that any of them applies to -- seed-major, not row-major -- a seed at `done` or `rejected` having no row whatever its tests say, and a seed whose colour is unknown having none either. (5) At `open`: `write the Goal of <seed>; status spec`. (6) At `spec` or `building` with no test naming it: `write red tests naming seed: <seed>; commit them; status building`. (7) At `spec`, named by tests: `set <seed> to building: its tests name it`. (8) Its tests red: `build <seed>: factory-build <root> <branch> docs/seeds/<seed>.md`, `<branch>` the branch of `Facts` and the literal `<branch>` when that is `None`, followed by ` -- detach first: git switch --detach` when `attached`. (9) Its tests green and no build since the tag names it: `<seed> is green with no build: commit the work with a Built-By trailer, or set it done and say why`. (10) Green, built, the latest build unreviewed: `review build <stamp> of <seed>: brief a subagent, write docs/reviews/<stamp>.md`. (11) Green, built, reviewed, not `done`: `set <seed> to done`. (12) Every seed done or rejected: the first of `revise AGENTS.md: unchanged since <tag>`, `write a ## Changelog bullet in docs/versions/<version>.md`, `commit: <the uncommitted paths>`, else `release: uv run gate.py release <version>`. (13) Last, and only when nothing above applied because a fact it needed is unknown: `nothing to do that is known: <the first line of unknown>`. With nothing unknown, one of the twelve rows applies to every state, and the table has no other row and `next_step` no other fallback.

**`repo.py` gains the readers the loop needs**, each raising `RepoError` where git cannot answer, so that `loop.py` reads git through `repo` alone: `toplevel(root)`, the checkout's top level as `listing` asks it, `None` for a directory that is no checkout or that git answers about another top level for; `head(root)`; `branch(root)`, `None` when detached; `branches_at(root, commit)`, the branches whose tip is the commit; `subject(root, commit)`; `distance(root, since, to)`, how many commits `to` is past `since`, `None` when `since` is no ancestor; `ls_remote(root, remote, ref, timeout)`, the hash the remote names for the ref; and `run_tests(root, ids, timeout)`, `green` or `red` from `python -m unittest <id>...` in the root, raising when the run could not happen or timed out, so `loop.py` holds no `subprocess`, no `ast`, no `read_text`, no `glob`, and no `repo.git(...)` of its own. `gather` reads the Vault once and passes it to the gate, and for itself reads the tags once and the commits since the tag once with each one's subject and trailers, asking nothing twice; the gate's own check reads the tags and the builds a second time for its own problems, and that is the gate's to keep, not the loop's to work around.

**`gate.py next`.** `USAGE` gains `next`; `main` imports `loop` when asked for it, so the gate's readers do not import their reader's reader, and prints `next: <text>` then the `unknown:` lines, exit 0; it prints the gate's usage and exits 2 as the other commands do when misused. Nothing is written anywhere: `next` reads, runs the seed's tests, and says.

**After the second review.** The installed product's version is the host's fact and not the repository's: `host.py`, a module of one reader, `instance()`, answering `Instance(version, error)` from `uv tool list` run as three words, escapes stripped, `version` `None` with the words of what went wrong and never an exception; `repo.py` has no reader of the host. A fact that is `None` is never read as a value: rows 3, 4 and 12 need `in_flight`, `dirty`, `agents_changed` and `bullets` known, a seed's rows need its `colour` and `builds` known -- `builds` is `None` with a line when the commits could not be listed -- and after a tag each act whose fact is `None` is skipped alike, the `else` holding. `after_tag` is git's distance and nothing else: the next version's note, written or committed, hides no act. Every `None` has its line, named for the field, and a directory that is no checkout has one per git fact and asks the enclosing repository nothing; a listing that failed inside a checkout says so and leaves the head, the tags and the rest as read. A detached head at the tip of two branches is `branch` `None` with a line naming them. A seed at `done` or `rejected` has its tests not run. `repo.run_stamp(entry)` is the one reading of a `Built-By` trailer's run, the gate's and the loop's, `None` for anything but `run <stamp>` whole; `vault.names_seed(text, name)` the one reading of `seed: <name>`. A test's seed is the innermost docstring that names one: a method whose docstring names a seed is that seed's, and running its class for the seed the class names must leave that method out -- found live, where a class naming the split's seed held a method naming this one, and the split read red for it. `RULES` is the only dispatch -- no if-chain beside it deciding a seed's row, no tuple naming the rows a second time -- and `next_step` has no fallback after the table. `ruff check` passes on `loop.py`, `repo.py` and `host.py`.

**Held by tests.** `test_loop.py`: every row, with a `Facts` built by hand and `next_step` answering that row and no other, in the table's order; `gather` over the gate tests' own fixture -- `v0.2` in flight, `b` at `spec` -- answering row 5, then row 7 once a red test names `b`, then row 8 or 9 as the fixture is advanced; an unknown mirror printed and skipped; `gate.py next` printing `next: ` first. What must not change: `check`, `render` and `release` exactly as they are, and every reader of `vault.py` and `repo.py` as its tests hold it.
