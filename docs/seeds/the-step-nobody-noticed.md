## Evidence

2026-09-21, the morning after v0.19 shipped, read by the attended agent cold from `AGENTS.md`. `README.md` opened with `v0.14`, counted 212 tests of 321, named neither `reviewer.py` nor `catch.py`, and described caps two versions gone; nothing reads it, and the loop's step 6 does not name it. `installation-and-surfaces`, value 5, sat `open` and unassigned across five versions with its Idea delivered by v0.15. `the-catch-rate-that-decides-the-role` sat at `spec` for a day with 23 green tests naming it, because `gate.py:624` holds status in one direction. On release day `git merge --ff-only v0.19` took the tag, one commit short of the branch, past a one-line warning. Everything the gate holds -- the vault's shape, a seed's fields, `Built-By`, review judgements, changelog bullets, `AGENTS.md` changed since the tag -- had not drifted. Everything that drifted was held by prose.

The owner, the same day, named two causes beyond the prose itself. A long session is compressed round after round, and an agent's performance deteriorates with each; and the workflow exists only as text, with nothing guiding the agent to the next step, so whether a step is taken is fully dependent on whether the instruction is noticed in context. The factory already treats its own roles this way: a builder and a reviewer are cold sessions, given what they need and nothing to remember.

The architectural round, 2026-09-21. Three shapes were put to the owner: rules as data over one snapshot of facts in a module of its own, with the gate's readers made public; the same after splitting `gate.py` first; or more imperative checks inside `gate.py`. The owner chose the split first, as [[the-gate-in-three]], and the rules-as-data shape after it. Two smaller questions were put and not answered, and are decided here as assumptions the owner may overturn at review: the colour of a seed's tests is learned by running those tests alone, seconds rather than the suite's minute; and the acts after a tag -- the changelog's commit, `main`, the mirror, the instance -- are rows of the loop, each read fail-soft.

Five builds and two reviews later, 2026-09-21: the first build's review found thirteen things and the second's ten, most of them the loop guessing where the Goal said `None`, several of them taught by the attended agent's own fixtures -- a shim that answered `uv "tool list"`, a repository whose head sits at its tag, a test that committed a clean tree. The fifth build read everything and wrote nothing before the cap. This Goal is the spec rewritten whole from what those rounds settled, in place of a spec and three afterthoughts.

## Goal

The next step of the loop is derived and printed, never remembered.

**Build order, because a cold run that reads everything first spends its cap before its first write -- two runs did.** Take the stages in order; a stage is done when its tests are green; a run that has finished a stage and cannot finish the next reports with the check red, and the next run starts from its tree. Stage one, reading only `repo.py`, `vault.py`, `test_repo.py`, `test_vault.py` and `test_host.py`: `host.py` with `instance()`; `repo.run_stamp`; `repo.toplevel` and every reader of `repo.py` raising for a root that is not its own top level; `vault.names_seed`; `repo.instance_version` gone. Stage two, reading `loop.py` and `test_loop.py`'s `RulesTest`: the table -- unknown facts skipping their rows, one dispatch, no fallback. Stage three, `loop.py` and `test_loop.py`'s `GatherTest`: `gather` on the readers. Read `gate.py` only when a test sends you there.

**Shape.** `loop.py` holds one snapshot and one table. `Facts` is a frozen dataclass gathered once by `gather(root)` through `vault`, `repo` and `host` and through nothing else: `loop.py` has no `subprocess`, no `ast`, no `read_text`, no `open(`, no `.glob(` and no `repo.git(` of its own. `RULES` is an ordered tuple of thirteen `Rule(name, applies, step)`, each `applies` a predicate over `Facts` and each `step` a function of `Facts` returning the text to print; `next_step(facts) -> Step(name, text)` is the first rule that applies, top to bottom, and there is no other dispatch -- no if-chain beside the table deciding a seed's row, no tuple naming the rows a second time -- and no fallback after it. `render(facts)` is `next: <text>` and then `unknown: <line>` for each line of `unknown`. Rules as data over a functional core; the reading at the edge.

**A fact that could not be read is `None`, with one line in `unknown` named for the field, `<field>: <why>`; it is never a value that means something else, and a row that needs it is skipped.** So: rows 3, 4 and 12 need `in_flight`, `dirty`, `agents_changed` and `bullets` known; a seed's rows need its `colour` and `builds` known; after a tag each act whose fact is `None` is skipped alike, the `else` holding. A directory that is no git checkout, or one git answers about another top level for, has every git fact `None` with one line each and asks the enclosing repository nothing -- `repo`'s readers answer only for a root that is its own top level, and raise `RepoError` otherwise. A listing that failed inside a checkout says so, and leaves the head, the tags and the rest as read.

**`Facts`, by field.** `root`. `problems`: the gate's own, `gate.problems_check(root, vault)` over the one Vault `gather` read, as the lines the gate prints. `tags`, `highest`. `head`. `branch` and `attached`: the branch the head is on and `True`; detached, the one branch whose tip is the head and `False`; `None` and `False` with a line when there is none or more than one. `in_flight`: the one version note whose name is no tag, `None` when there is none, and `None` with a line when the tags are unknown. `seeds`: one `SeedFacts` per seed whose `version` is the version in flight, in name order -- `name`; `status`; `tests`, the ids run for it, from `repo.tests(root).found` and `vault.names_seed`: each method whose docstring names it, and each method of a class whose docstring names it that names no other seed itself -- a test's seed is the innermost docstring that names one, so a method naming a seed is that seed's and not its class's, and a class id is never run whole; `colour`, `green` or `red` from `repo.run_tests` over exactly those ids, `none` when no test names it, `None` with a line when the run could not happen, and not run at all for a seed at `done` or `rejected`; `builds`, one `Build(commit, stamp)` per commit since the highest tag whose subject is the seed's name and whose `Built-By` trailer `repo.run_stamp` reads a stamp from, oldest first, `None` with a line when they could not be listed; `reviewed`, whether the Vault holds `docs/reviews/<stamp>.md` for the latest build, `None` with no build. `agents_changed`, `bullets`, `dirty`: whether `AGENTS.md` changed since the highest tag, whether the version note has a changelog bullet, the uncommitted paths. `after_tag`: whether the highest tag is at the head or exactly one commit behind it, by `repo.distance` and nothing else -- a version in flight, a note written or committed, changes it not. `changelog_current`: whether `CHANGELOG.md`'s first `## ` heading names the highest tag whole, `False` when the file is missing, `None` with a line when it cannot be read. `main_at_head`. `mirror_at_head`: whether `origin`'s `main` is at the head, asked through `repo.ls_remote` with a timeout and only when `after_tag`, `None` with no line before a tag since no row reads it then. `instance_version`: `host.instance().version`. `unknown`: the lines.

**The rows, in this order.** (1) The gate has problems: `fix: <the first, as the gate prints it>` -- whatever else is true, after a tag as before one. (2) After a tag, the first of: `commit CHANGELOG.md`; `fast-forward main: git merge --ff-only heads/<tag>`; `push: git push origin main --tags`; `install: uv tool install --reinstall dist/factory-<number>-py3-none-any.whl`, `<number>` the tag without its `v`; else `<tag> is out: open the next version`. (3) No version in flight: `open a version: write docs/versions/vX.md and set version: vX on a seed`. (4) The version in flight names no seed: `promote a seed to <version>`. Rows 5 to 11 are a seed's; the seed stepped is the first in name order that any of them applies to -- seed-major -- a seed at `done` or `rejected` having none, and a seed whose colour or builds are unknown having none. (5) At `open`: `write the Goal of <seed>; status spec`. (6) At `spec` or `building` with no test naming it: `write red tests naming seed: <seed>; commit them; status building`. (7) At `spec`, named by tests: `set <seed> to building: its tests name it`. (8) Tests red: `build <seed>: factory-build <root> <branch> docs/seeds/<seed>.md`, `<branch>` the branch of `Facts` and the literal `<branch>` when that is `None`, then ` -- detach first: git switch --detach` when `attached`. (9) Green, no build: `<seed> is green with no build: commit the work with a Built-By trailer, or set it done and say why`. (10) Green, built, the latest build unreviewed: `review build <stamp> of <seed>: brief a subagent, write docs/reviews/<stamp>.md`. (11) Green, built, reviewed, not `done`: `set <seed> to done`. (12) Every seed done or rejected, the first of: `revise AGENTS.md: unchanged since <tag>`; `write a ## Changelog bullet in docs/versions/<version>.md`; `commit: <the uncommitted paths>`; else `release: uv run gate.py release <version>`. (13) Only when nothing above applied because a fact it needed is unknown: `nothing to do that is known: <the first line of unknown>`. With nothing unknown, one of the twelve applies to every state.

**What the modules gain.** `repo.py`: `toplevel(root)`, the checkout's top level or `None`; `head(root)`; `branch(root)`, `None` when detached; `branches_at(root, commit)`; `subject(root, commit)`; `distance(root, since, to)`, `None` when `since` is no ancestor; `ls_remote(root, remote, ref, timeout)`; `run_tests(root, ids, timeout)`, `green` or `red`, raising when the run could not happen or timed out; `run_stamp(entry)`, the one reading of a `Built-By` trailer's run, the gate's and the loop's, `None` for anything but `run <stamp>` whole; and every reader raising `RepoError` where git cannot answer or the root is not its own top level. `vault.py`: `names_seed(text, name)`, the one reading of `seed: <name>`. `host.py`, a module of one reader: `instance()` answering `Instance(version, error)` from `uv tool list` run as three words, escapes stripped, `version` `None` and the words of what went wrong when there is no factory or no uv, never an exception; `repo.py` reads nothing of the host. `gate.py next`: `USAGE` gains `next`; `main` imports `loop` when asked for it and prints `render`, exit 0; usage and exit 2 when misused; nothing written anywhere.

**Formatting.** `ruff check` passes on `loop.py`, `repo.py`, `vault.py` and `host.py`; `ruff format` is the attended agent's act after.

**Held by tests.** `test_loop.py`: every row over a `Facts` built by hand, in the table's order; unknown facts skipping their rows; `gather` over the gate tests' fixture, two commits past its tag, advanced from red tests to release and past a tag; the enclosing repository, the detached head, the failed listing, the unrunnable tests, the trailer-less commit, the junk stamp, the done seed's tests not run; `gate.py next` writing nothing. `test_repo.py`, `test_vault.py` and `test_host.py` hold the readers. What must not change: `check`, `render` and `release` as they are, and every reader of `vault.py` and `repo.py` as its tests hold it.
